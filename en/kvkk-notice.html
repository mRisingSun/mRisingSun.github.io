We do not process names, Turkish ID numbers, phone numbers, location or special categories of personal data. Game progress is stored on your device. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, a copy is backed up to Google's Play Games saved games service; it is not transferred to our servers.

3. Method of collection

Data is collected electronically and automatically through the mobile app. Crash and analytics data are collected only after you allow them on the in-game consent screen or under Settings. Email data is collected when you write to us.

4. Transfers

Data is shared only for the purposes above, with these providers:

  • Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry), USA (data hosted in the EU): crash reports;
  • GameAnalytics ApS, Denmark: analytics;
  • Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.: Google Play (distribution and in-app payments), Google Play Games (cloud save) and AdMob (ads).

These are transfers abroad under KVKK Art. 9. They rely on your explicit consent and/or standard contracts with the providers. Data may also be disclosed to authorised public bodies when the law requires it.

5. Retention

Retention periods:

  • crash reports: up to 90 days;
  • raw analytics data: up to for the period stated in GameAnalytics' privacy policy;
  • correspondence: 2 years after the request is closed;
  • purchase records: as tax law requires.

After that, the data is deleted or anonymised.

6. Your rights (KVKK Art. 11)

You may ask the data controller to:

  • tell you whether your data is processed, and give you information about it;
  • tell you the purpose of processing and whether the data is used for that purpose;
  • name the third parties, in Turkey or abroad, that receive your data;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete or destroy data under the conditions of Art. 7;
  • notify third parties of these corrections or deletions;
  • reconsider a result against you that comes solely from automated analysis;
  • compensate damage caused by unlawful processing.

You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Crash Reports / Analytics.

7. Applications

Send requests to bagstion.destek@gmail.com. We answer free of charge within 30 days. For deletion, see the Data Deletion page.
