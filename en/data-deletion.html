Data Deletion Request

Bagstion — developer: mRS Studio

Bagstion has no user accounts. You never sign in with a name or email address, and your progress is stored on your device. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, a copy also lives in your Play Games saved games (see 1b below). The only data that may exist on servers is the crash reports and analytics events sent with your consent. These are linked to a random installation ID, not to you personally.

1. Delete data on your device

  1. In the Game, tap Settings → Reset Progress twice. This erases all progress and your privacy choices. Or:
  2. Uninstall the app. Android also removes all of its local data.

Important: if you also want server-side data deleted, write down the ID shown on the Settings screen before you reset or uninstall. A reset creates a new ID, and the old one is not kept.

1b. Delete the cloud save (Google Play Games)

Google stores the cloud copy in your Play Games account. It never reaches us, so you delete it directly:

  • Only this game's cloud save: Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Bagstion → Delete hidden app data.
  • All your Play Games data: Play Games app → Profile → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data (removes your Play Games data for every game).

Note: Reset Progress in the game does not delete the cloud copy immediately; it is replaced when your first new progress is backed up. Until then you can bring the old progress back with Settings → Restore from cloud.

2. Stop data collection

In the Game, open Settings and switch off Crash Reports and Analytics. Sending stops immediately.

3. Request deletion of server-side data

Email bagstion.destek@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request — Bagstion". Include:

  • your device model and roughly when you installed the Game;
  • your installation ID: the 12-character code shown as "Kimlik: …" at the bottom of the Settings screen (for example 3FA91C07B2DE).

We will complete your request within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataOn request
Cloud save (Google Play Games)You delete it with the steps in 1b above; we hold no copy on our servers.
Crash reports (Sentry)Reports matching your ID are deleted. All other reports are deleted automatically within 90 days.
Analytics events (GameAnalytics)We ask the provider to delete events matching your ID. Aggregated statistics that cannot identify anyone (for example "share of players who cleared stage 3") may be kept.
Email correspondenceDeleted. A short record that the request was fulfilled may be kept for for the period stated in GameAnalytics' privacy policy to meet legal obligations.
Purchase recordsPurchase tokens are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app. Resetting your progress does not remove your purchases (permanent items such as the ad-free VIP can be restored from your Google Play account). Records that tax law requires us to keep are deleted when the legal retention period ends.

Random IDs cannot be linked to a person. If you do not have your ID, we may not be able to find your server-side data, and we will tell you so. That data is still deleted automatically when the retention periods above end.
