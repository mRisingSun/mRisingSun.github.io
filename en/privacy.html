Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15.11.2026 • Last updated: 15.11.2026

1. Who we are

The mobile game Bagstion (the "Game") is developed and published by mRS Studio ("we"). We are the data controller for your personal data.

Contact: bagstion.destek@gmail.com

2. In short

  • You do not need an account to play. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, a copy is backed up to your Google Play Games saved games so it is not lost when you change phones (see 3.2).
  • Crash reports and gameplay analytics are optional. The Game asks you on first launch, and if you decline, nothing is sent. You can change your choice at any time under Settings.
  • We do not sell your data.

3. Data we process

3.1 Game data that stays on your device

Your progress (levels, resources, settings and privacy choices) is kept in the app's private storage on your device and is never sent to us. It is erased when you uninstall the app or use Settings → Reset Progress.

3.2 Cloud save (optional): Google Play Games

If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, the game signs in to Play Games automatically and uploads a copy of your progress to the Google Play Games Saved Games service. This lets you get your progress back after changing phones or reinstalling. If you are not signed in to Play Games, nothing is uploaded and your save stays only on your device.

  • What is uploaded: your save file (levels, resources, quest and reward records, purchase records, game settings), a random device ID generated for this installation, and the time of the save.
  • Google stores the copy linked to your Play Games account and processes your Play Games player ID and account data to provide the service. We do not receive or store your name, email address or Play Games player ID on our own servers.
  • Cloud save events (backed up, restored, error) are sent to analytics only if you consented to analytics.

You can see the connection and backup status under Settings → Google Play Games and use "Back up now" and "Restore from cloud" there. The service is provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd. under Google's terms of service and privacy policy.

3.2b Crash reports (optional): Sentry

If you consent, the Game sends a report when it crashes or hits an error, so we can find and fix the problem. A report contains:

  • the error message, the code lines involved and the last lines of the game log;
  • device model, OS version, screen and memory information, and game version;
  • the game steps just before the error (for example "stage started" or "chest opened");
  • a randomly generated installation ID, which is shown at the bottom of the Settings screen.

This service is provided by Functional Software, Inc. ("Sentry", USA). We choose to host this data in Sentry's EU (Germany) data region and have turned off IP address storage. We never add your name or contact details to a report.

3.3 Gameplay analytics (optional): GameAnalytics

If you consent, the Game sends gameplay statistics that help us see which parts are too hard or not fun:

  • when a session starts and how long it lasts;
  • tutorial steps, stage start and result (world, stage, difficulty, wave reached, duration), boss encounters, chest openings and unlocked features;
  • where in-game currencies (gold, gems) are earned and spent;
  • device model, OS, language and approximate country;
  • the random installation ID. We do not use your advertising ID (Google Advertising ID).

This service is provided by GameAnalytics ApS (Denmark). Country is derived from your IP address. GameAnalytics' own privacy policy explains how it handles IP addresses.

3.4 Advertising: Google AdMob

A future version of the Game may offer optional rewarded ads, for example watching an ad to earn a bonus. When that happens:

  • Google LLC's AdMob service will show the ads, and it processes data such as the advertising ID, IP address and device information;
  • players in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland will be asked through Google's consent tool (UMP / IAB TCF) before ads are personalised; players who decline will see non-personalised ads only;
  • for players in Turkey, personalised ads and transfers abroad will require explicit consent;
  • we will update this policy and announce the change in the Game.

3.5 In-app purchases: Google Play

The Game offers optional in-app purchases: gem packs, a starter pack, a monthly card, the Royal Pass and an ad-free VIP. Google Play handles payments; we never receive your card or bank details. To deliver the item and to make sure the same purchase is never processed twice, the product ID, order number and purchase token provided by Google Play are stored only in the game save on your device. We do not send them to a server of our own; we do not run one. If you allowed analytics, purchase steps (for example purchase started, completed or cancelled; the product ID and its price tier) are sent to GameAnalytics as pseudonymous analytics events. Your purchase history and refund requests are managed in your Google Play account. Records required by law (for example for tax) are kept by Google Play and, where needed, by us for the period the law sets.

Chest contents in the Game are random. The odds are shown in the Game, on the OLASILIKLAR (odds) screen in the chest room. Chests are never sold directly for real money.

3.6 Emails you send us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to answer your request, and we keep them for up to 2 years after the request is closed.

7. Children

The Game is designed for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe this has happened, contact us and we will delete the data.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR and KVKK Art. 11 you have the right to:

  • access your data;
  • have it corrected or erased;
  • restrict or object to processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time;
  • lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority or, in Turkey, the Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK).

Withdraw consent: in the Game, switch off Settings → Crash Reports / Analytics. Sending stops immediately.

Request deletion: follow the steps on the Data Deletion page.

Send requests to bagstion.destek@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

9. Security

Data is sent to providers over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Our provider accounts are protected with two-factor authentication, and only the developer uses them.

10. Changes

We will update this policy when we add a new service, such as ads or purchases. We will announce material changes in the Game and ask for your consent again where required.
